7. The case: ℛenova Press as of 24 November 2026

7.1. What ℛenova Press is

ℛenova Press is the publishing imprint of Rey Filósofo and SERESARTE. Its legal owner is Carlos Jonathan González Rodríguez. Its tagline, written in its own storekit, is "Living thought in the form of a work", and its own definition, in the portal, is: "Renova as thought, Press as public form and SERESARTE as creative community". Its symbol is the Portal: three open arches. Its six colours are Black (#111111), White (#FFFFFF), Ivory (#F7F3EC), Deep Charcoal (#1F1F1F), Antique Gold (#B98A3C) and Soft Taupe (#B9B1A3). Its registry defines it as an "editorial imprint for books, essays, dossiers, philosophical work and editorial products". All of the above is recorded in the imprint's files analysed on 19 November 2026.

The corpus that sustains Press is the ℛenova — Fundamento space: 54 works and 11,724 verified pages. The Renova podcast is live on Spotify with four episodes, and its fifth episode is scheduled for 30 November 2026 [scheduled date according to the working documents; to be verified when it arrives]. The YouTube channel @reyfilosofo.seresarte has had its first video live since 23 November 2026. Press does not begin on paper: it begins in voice and in image, and afterwards becomes a book. That is the third culture in action: the same thought in audio, in video and on the page.

7.6. Reading the case through Holland's seven elements

Read through Holland, the case is a manual. Aggregation: 140 records grouped into collections and series, which act as meta-agents when treated as a whole; the 314 bios of the ℛenova swarm are the same phenomenon at another scale. Tagging: the Portal, prices as tags, Stripe links as identifiers that filter and specialise. Non-linearity: twelve charges in almost two months and then a five-day window with zero payments and $419 MXN of intent: the behaviour of the aggregate is not the sum of its parts. Flow: money, files and attention circulate through networks that appear and disappear; the task "Ventas y entregas · SERESARTE Ω" is deactivated and keeps an old Stripe reference, and the Orcas service declares an automatic email that is not configured: the flow is broken at that node and must be repaired before resuming it. Diversity: each work occupies a niche defined by its interactions; BACHE, with no master and no rights, is an empty niche that still sustains no agent. Internal modelling: CENTRO, which evaluates before acting, is the imprint's internal model, and this essay is its internal model in the form of a text. Building blocks: from limited samples — twelve charges, three sessions, one window — regularities are found without pretending that the sample is the universe. And the edge of chaos, the point between chaos and stasis where adaptation and evolution are most likely to take place, is exactly where Press stands: stable enough to store information — 140 records, 54 works, a catalogue — and evanescent enough to transmit it — a podcast, a video, a newly installed blog —. Evolution, as the thesis says, always operates there.

Conclusions: theses

After this panoramic journey through the crisis of rationality, the two cultures, chaos, dissipative structures, complex adaptive systems, emergence and the documentary case of ℛenova Press, we come to endorse a series of theses that sum up the sense of our research. They are formulated in the manner of Vera Ortiz, in his Conclusiones to "La sensibilidad posmoderna y el pensamiento de la crisis": phrases in quotation marks, one per line, first the general ones and then those on philosophy. The transcription is literal from the source files and preserves their spelling; where the original sentence is syntactically irregular, it is quoted as it stands, without polishing it, because polishing a quotation is falsifying it. The theses of Vera Ortiz are therefore quoted below in the original Spanish; the theses of this essay follow in English.

General theses (Vera Ortiz)

  • «Se torna imperativo incorporar los descubrimientos técnicos y metodológicos de las ciencias de la complejidad para una si acaso se pudiera obtener comprensión optima de nuestro tiempo presente».
  • «Las ciencias de la complejidad representan tanto una hiperespecialización de las ciencias clásicas como de las ciencias del siglo XX».
  • «La cosmovisión derivada de las ciencias de la complejidad implica el abandono de las fronteras infranqueables entre las ciencias naturales y las ciencias sociales».
  • «Las ciencias sociales quieren permanecer –a cierto nivel- presas de la necesidad de construcción de Teorías del Todo que (en nuestros tiempos postmodernos) es inexorable cuestionar».
  • «La globalización –como proceso general de mutaciones tecnoeconómicas, político sociales, fonológicas y sensoriales- hunde sus raíces en los albores del capitalismo cuando se gesta la emergencia del mercado mundial».
  • «La globalización es inevitable, y quizá biológica, aunque se puede luchar contra sus consecuencias más nefastas; para ello resulta indispensable la organización autogestiva lato sensu; será autopoiesis».
  • «La posmodernidad no fue solamente una moda intelectual. Más aún, se convirtió en la sensibilidad fundante de nuestra época».
  • «La socialidad científica posmoderna requiere de un envite paradojal e incluso esquizoide: apuesta al abandono parcial –de hecho, casi total e individual- de la razón clásica y, a su vez, somete unilateral a las mayorías otras, a la racionalidad exclusiva del imperio del capital. Proteicamente». [Transcripción literal del archivo; la sintaxis es la del original.]

  • «El tiempo de la filosofía ha pasado ya».
  • «Si hoy día desapareciese la filosofía del panorama intelectual todo seguiría igual aunque habría unos cuantos desempleados».
  • «La filosofía perdió las baterías críticas que quizás algún día poseyó para integrarse por completo al establecimiento y devenir una disciplina dogmática [...]».
  • «La filosofía dejo de ser asunto de gente pensante ahora es materia de especialistas». [sic]
  • «La filosofía se ha tornado una mera hagiografía una simple exégesis más preocupada por lo que ocurre con los libros que lo que ocurre con el mundo real». [sic]
  • «La filosofía es ya el comentario del comentario [...]».
  • «La filosofía ha perdido contacto con la realidad ahora su realidad se reducen los textos». [sic]
  • «La filosofía ha perdido el humor el júbilo que acaso algún día la caracterizó [...]». [sic]
  • «La filosofía se ha convertido en una actividad acomodaticia más preocupada por seguir las formalidades y cumplir las normas que por cuestionar al mundo».
  • «La filosofía ha llegado hacer un simulacro de saber [...]». [sic]
  • «De ser crítica del orden la filosofía ha pasado a ser garante del equilibrio y el orden social una teoría políticamente correcta». [sic]
  • «La filosofía vive hoy del desencanto [...]».
  • «Con ello la filosofía se ha convertido en una graciosa actividad en la que [...] se establecen diálogos de sordos». [sic; el original intercala palabras ilegibles en la transcripción]

The coincidence this essay points out, with express attribution to Vera Ortiz, is that his first thesis on philosophy — "El tiempo de la filosofía ha pasado ya" ("The time of philosophy has already passed") — is also Rey Filósofo's motto. In Vera Ortiz it is a diagnosis of closure: philosophy lost its critical batteries, its capacity for wonder, its humour, its negativity and its contact with the real world, and became commentary on commentary. In Rey it is a watchword of opening: if the time of philosophy has already passed, then philosophy must be made again — in books, in essays, in dossiers, in works that can be read, touched, shared and read again — and ℛenova Press is the place where it is made. The same sentence, read in two directions, is a diagnosis and a programme. This essay does not resolve that tension: it documents it and leaves it as a task.

Theses of this essay (ℛenova Press)

  • "A publishing house is a complex adaptive system: its behaviour is more than the sum of the behaviours of its records, its products and its readers."
  • "The map is not the territory: a published record does not prove a delivery, a purchase link does not prove a payment, and a simulation does not prove external validation."
  • "Editorial systems are computationally irreducible: their future state can only be anticipated by following their dynamics step by step, sale by sale, session by session."
  • "Emergence is not a spell: nothing can be concluded from the notion of complexity in itself, and no property — a sale, a community, a validation — arises from the mere invocation of the word complexity."
  • "The edge of chaos is the publishing house's place: stable enough to store information and evanescent enough to transmit it."
  • "Verify before believing, model before acting and publish in batches is the method of complexity applied to publishing."
  • "Philosophy, if it is to become living thought again, must recover its capacity for wonder, its negativity, its humour and its contact with the real world; ℛenova Press is the place where that recovery is put to the test, work by work."

ℛenova says A → A′: everything that exists can become something new. So can a publishing house. So can philosophy.
