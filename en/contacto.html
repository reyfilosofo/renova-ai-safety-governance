Who Rey Filósofo is

Carlos Jonathan González Rodríguez, Rey Filósofo, is a philosopher (BA in Philosophy from the Universidad del Claustro de Sor Juana), creator of ℛenova — the philosophy of transformation and foundational sensitivity — and founder of SERESARTE and Fundación SERESARTE. He works as an artificial intelligence researcher and ethicist, and as a creative and editorial director.

What ℛenova Press is

ℛenova Press is the publishing imprint of Rey Filósofo and SERESARTE: "Renova as thought, Press as public form and SERESARTE as creative community." It publishes living thought in the form of a work — books, essays, dossiers and works — in Spanish, from Mexico and Latin America. Its symbol is the Portal: three open arches. A portal is not looked at: it is crossed.

CUADERNOS ℛ is its public working notebook: what Press has already done, explained calmly.
