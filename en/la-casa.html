The house

ℛenova Press is the publishing imprint of Rey Filósofo and SERESARTE. Its own definition: "Renova as thought, Press as public form and SERESARTE as creative community."

Its symbol is the Portal: three open arches. A portal is not looked at: it is crossed.

Three editorial lines

  • A house of poetry and literature.
  • A house of thought.
  • A house of art and culture.

The imprint publishes philosophical and editorial work in Spanish, in Mexican pesos, with the reader in Mexico and Latin America at its centre. Its background corpus is the ℛenova — Fundamento space, with 54 works and 11,724 pages.
