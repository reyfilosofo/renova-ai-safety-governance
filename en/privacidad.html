Privacy

Last updated: 24 November 2026.

CUADERNOS ℛ is the ℛenova Press blog. This site is a static web page, hosted on GitHub Pages, and is designed not to track you.

What this site does NOT do

  • It uses no first-party cookies and no tracking. There are no cookies from this site, no first-party analytics installed and no tracking pixels.
  • It collects no personal data. There are no forms, no user accounts and no comments on the site.
  • It does not sell or share data, because it collects none.

External links

This site links to external services, which have their own privacy policies and may use cookies when you visit them:

  • Stripe (book purchase links): processes payments. ℛenova Press does not see or store your card details.
  • Wix and ChatGPT Sites stores (ℛenova Press): showcases of the catalogue.
  • Substack (email subscription): if you subscribe there, your data is managed by Substack, not by this blog.
  • GitHub Pages (hosting): GitHub may record technical access data (such as your IP address) in its server logs, under its own policy.

Contact

If you write to jonathan@seresarte.org or via WhatsApp Business, your data is used only to reply to you. It is not shared with third parties or added to any list without your consent.

To exercise your rights over your data (access, rectification or deletion of a message you have sent us), write to jonathan@seresarte.org.

If advertising (for example, Google AdSense) or analytics are added in future, this page will be updated beforehand, explaining which cookies are used and how to disable them.
